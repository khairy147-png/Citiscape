Executive Summary & Positioning Statement

This opening page clarifies what already exists in policy and where the proposed enhancements strengthen the same control environment without replacing it.

Chapter 1

Current Baseline Controls Already in Place

  • Diesel: demand planning, PR / PO authorization, intake verification, issuance form, status gate, and manager reconciliation already exist in the diesel control process.
  • Equipment: equipment requisition, owned-first availability check, outsourced document verification, receiving inspection, HSE release, timesheet maintenance, PM approval, and Logistics verification already exist in the current policy control chain.
  • Outsourced Manpower: legality verification, camp admission, attendance recording, Project review, PM approval, Logistics verification, and off-hire / transfer governance already exist in the manpower control baseline.
  • Project Control Governance: budget validation is recognized as a necessary front-end governance gate to confirm whether demand is within BOQ / budget or requires variation handling before operational execution.

Enhancement Proposal Focus

  • Add a more explicit executive-facing layer that visually distinguishes baseline controls from enhancement actions.
  • Consolidate Diesel, Equipment, and Manpower into one navigation model to improve management review and audit readiness.
  • Introduce exception visibility: idle assets, breakdowns, expiry risk, pending PM approvals, pending Logistics verifications, and variation / budget hold status.
  • Keep the current policy flow intact while strengthening monitoring, decision speed, and accountability across Project Control, Projects, Logistics, and management.
